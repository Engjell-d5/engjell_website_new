# "Great service is built on clarity" — absorbed, not published

Recovered from the old WordPress install (post 1661, drafted 2024-10-09,
370 words, never published on either site). Raw body kept in
`great-service-is-built-on-clarity.recovered.html` for reference.

**Decision: folded into the pillar rather than published as its own post.**

## Why

At 370 words it was roughly half the length of a normal post here, and it
overlapped heavily with ground already covered. Its strongest claim, that
clarity builds trust and trust is the currency of long-term relationships, is
close to the argument in `kindness-as-the-ultimate-business-hack`, and its
process material now lives in the pillar's systems sections. Published
standalone it would have competed with the pillar for the same search intent
while saying less.

More usefully, it answered a question the pillar was raising and dropping. "What
growth actually came from" argued that word of mouth only works if the work is
genuinely good, then moved straight on without ever defining what good means.

## Where it went

End of "What growth actually came from", immediately after the line about
becoming a good communicator by becoming a good listener, and immediately before
the culture section.

Three of the original five points survived, compressed and rewritten in the
pillar's voice:

- **Clarity in the process** (was point 1)
- **Clarity in expectations** (was point 3)
- **Clarity when something breaks** (was point 4)

Dropped: point 2 (clarity in communication) because the listening line directly
above it already made that argument, and point 5 (clarity builds trust) as a
standalone heading, since it works better as the paragraph that ties the three
together and hands off to the culture section, where trust is already the theme.

Added one line that was not in the original, to make the handoff explicit:
"Which turns out to be the thing everything else in this piece depends on."

## Result

Pillar went from 2,417 to **2,686 words**, 14 minute read, still zero em dashes,
still 8 sections and 12 internal links.

The old draft is now fully represented and needs no further action. Nothing on
the old WordPress site remains unmigrated except site furniture pages.
