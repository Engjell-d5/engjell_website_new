# Excerpt rewrites

The excerpt is the meta description and the card summary. Google shows roughly
120–160 characters; below that you leave the snippet half-empty, and an excerpt
that describes *that* you wrote something rather than *what it says* wastes the
only sentence a searcher reads before deciding to click.

All 13 posts have excerpts. These three are too short or too self-referential to
work. Paste the replacements into the admin editor's Excerpt field.

---

## 1. The Silicon Valley Illusion

**Current** (~71 chars, and it describes the act of writing rather than the argument):
> Many years ago, I wrote an article called "The Silicon Valley Illusion."

**Replace with** (147 chars):
> Albanian founders keep importing a startup culture built for a place with unlimited venture capital. We don't have that luxury, and we don't need it.

## 2. Service vs Product

**Current** (50 chars — reads as the opening line, not a summary):
> A Service-based business sucks! Products are cool.

**Replace with** (155 chars):
> I spent years trying to pivot away from services, for three reasons I now know were wrong. Every business scales — most founders just can't scale theirs yet.

## 3. Make your journey purposeful

**Current** (66 chars):
> A good business should make money, a great business has a purpose.

**Replace with** (160 chars):
> A B2B service business will never claim it's changing the world. But providing careers that let young Albanians build their future here is a purpose worth having.
